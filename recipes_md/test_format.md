## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
- 2 cups **flour**, *sifted*
- 1 tbsp ***sugar*** (granulated)
- 3/4 cup cocoa powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt

### Wet Ingredients
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 1/2 cup [olive oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive_oil)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

### Spices & Seasonings
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 1/8 tsp cardamom

### Alternative Formatting Styles
- __Double underscore bold__
- _Single underscore italic_
- Check out ***[this site](https://btmonier.org)*** for more


## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375{{unit:degF}} (190{{unit:degC}}).
2. **Mix dry ingredients:** Combine flour, sugar, cocoa, baking soda, and salt.
3. *Whisk wet ingredients:* Beat eggs, then add milk, oil, and vanilla.
4. ***Combine carefully:*** Mix wet into dry until just combined.
5. Pour into greased pan (use butter {{unit:approx}} 1 tbsp).
6. Bake for 25-30 minutes at 375{{unit:deg}}.
7. Internal temperature should be {{unit:ge}} 190{{unit:degF}}.
8. Let cool for {{unit:ge}} 10 minutes before serving.
